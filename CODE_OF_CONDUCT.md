# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BNA UI a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps this community:

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Focus on constructive feedback
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them
- Be collaborative, and assume good faith
- Show empathy toward other community members

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
code review — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **ahmdabdelsamea@gmail.com**. Reports are reviewed
promptly and treated confidentially; you will not be asked to justify why the
behaviour bothered you.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
